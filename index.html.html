What If Your Wallet Could Actually Talk to You?

MantleIQ is an AI co-pilot built natively on Mantle that reads the chain, interprets what matters, and tells you what to do next — in plain language.

View Live Dashboard ↓

There's a version of DeFi that exists in people's heads before they ever try it. Fast. Efficient. Money working quietly in the background while you get on with your life. The promise is clean and the pitch is compelling — and for a long time, that pitch has been enough to get people in the door.

But getting in the door is not the same as staying.

I've watched too many people bridge funds to a new chain, land on a protocol, stare at a screen full of numbers, and feel immediately, genuinely lost. Not because they aren't smart. Not because the technology isn't real. But because nobody designed that moment for them. The interface assumed knowledge they didn't have. The data was all there — TVL, APY, liquidity depth — but data without context isn't information. It's noise. And most people, when they encounter enough noise, do the rational thing: they leave.

"The real barrier to DeFi adoption isn't gas fees or wallet setup or even volatility. It's that the space expects fluency it never teaches."

That's the gap I keep coming back to. Not the technical gap — Mantle's infrastructure handles that. The intelligence gap. The space between what's happening onchain and what a person actually needs to understand to make a confident decision. MantleIQ is my answer to that gap.

The Opportunity Is Already There

Mantle's DeFi ecosystem is real and growing fast. As of March 2026, Mantle's total DeFi TVL has crossed $755 million, up 230% in six months. Aave's deployment on Mantle alone crossed $1.34 billion in lending and borrowing activity — making it the third-largest Aave market globally. Bybit's Mantle Vault, powered by CIAN Protocol and Aave, crossed $150 million in assets under management. The mETH Protocol, Mantle's native liquid staking solution, holds over $1.1 billion in TVL and ranks among the top five LSTs globally.

The infrastructure is clearly working. The protocols are active. The liquidity is real. But the question that doesn't get asked enough is: how many people who show up actually understand what they're looking at?

An AI That Reads the Chain So You Don't Have To

When a user connects their wallet to MantleIQ, the first thing it does is take stock — assets held, current positions, idle capital, historical activity — and turn all of it into a plain-language briefing. Not a dashboard full of eighteen metrics. A clear, honest summary followed by a practical next step.

From there, MantleIQ monitors Mantle's transaction layer continuously — tracking liquidity shifts across Merchant Moe, Agni Finance, INIT Capital, and Lendle — and surfaces signals that are worth acting on. Not raw alerts, but interpreted ones. The kind that tell you why something is worth your attention, not just that it changed.

Built for Mantle, Not Just Built on It

Mantle's low transaction costs — averaging $0.002 per swap versus Ethereum's $5–15 range — make it economically viable to monitor onchain activity at the granularity MantleIQ needs. The tool reads directly from Mantle's deployed contracts: vault states, pool composition, live liquidity data. Every recommendation is grounded in what's actually happening right now.

For users in Mantle's LST ecosystem, MantleIQ speaks that language natively. mETH staking, cmETH restaking via EigenLayer, yield optimization — it knows the terrain because it's built inside it. The conversational interface is calibrated to Mantle's actual ecosystem, not generic DeFi advice that could have come from any chain.

The Compounding Effect of Smarter Users

A chain's growth is ultimately a function of how many people participate meaningfully over time — not just how many arrive. Retention comes from one thing more than anything else: people feeling like they understand what's happening and have some confidence in their decisions.

MantleIQ is a bet on that insight. Every user it helps onboard properly is a user who stays. Every trader it equips with better signals goes deeper. The compounding effect — more active users, deeper liquidity, higher volume — is the real value the tool creates. Mantle already has the infrastructure. MantleIQ gives it the intelligence layer to match.

The data is already onchain. The users are already showing up. The gap between them just needs to be closed.
